## Question 1

What is the starting point for making good architectural decisions?

- [ ] Immediately choosing the most popular solution.
- [x] A good understanding of the problem.
- [ ] Relying solely on past experiences.
- [ ] Ignoring all external influences.

<details>
<summary>Rationale</summary>
<p>

**Rationale:** Good understanding of the problem is the starting point for any solution finding. Popular solutions, past-only experiences, and ignoring influences all miss problem understanding's foundational role in effective decision-making.

</p>
</details>

## Question 2

Which of the following questions are crucial for understanding the problem when making architectural decisions?

- [ ] What is the project budget?
- [x] What exactly is the problem?
- [x] Why is it relevant to the architecture?
- [x] How will the decision affect the problem?
- [ ] Who will implement the solution?

<details>
<summary>Rationale</summary>
<p>

**Rationale:** Crucial questions are: what is the problem, why is it architecturally relevant, how will decisions affect it. Budget and implementers are project management concerns, not problem understanding questions.

</p>
</details>

## Question 3

What are some influencing factors beyond the problem that impact solution finding for architectural decisions?

- [ ] Personal preferences of the architect.
- [x] Constraints, quality goals, and risks addressed.
- [ ] The latest technology trends.
- [ ] The number of developers available.

<details>
<summary>Rationale</summary>
<p>

**Rationale:** Influencing factors include constraints, quality goals, and risks addressed. Personal preferences, technology trends, and developer count are secondary concerns—constraints and quality goals drive architectural decisions.

</p>
</details>

## Question 4

What is the purpose of making assumptions in architectural decision-making?

- [ ] To avoid making any decisions.
- [x] To deal with unknowns and identify risks.
- [ ] To simplify by ignoring complex details.
- [ ] To ensure the solution is always perfect.

<details>
<summary>Rationale</summary>
<p>

**Rationale:** Assumptions deal with unknowns and identify new risks emerging from decisions. Decision avoidance, ignoring complexity, and perfection guarantees all contradict assumptions' risk-identification and uncertainty-management purposes.

</p>
</details>

## Question 5

What is the benefit of considering alternative solutions when making architectural decisions?

- [ ] It makes decision-making longer and complicated.
- [x] It helps identify pros, cons, risks, alternatives.
- [ ] It guarantees first solution is always best.
- [ ] It eliminates the need for further evaluation.

<details>
<summary>Rationale</summary>
<p>

**Rationale:** Alternative consideration helps work out pros/cons, inspires ideas, identifies risks, and ensures comprehensive exploration. Complexity complaints, first-solution-best beliefs, and evaluation elimination all miss alternatives' value in thorough decision-making.

</p>
</details>
